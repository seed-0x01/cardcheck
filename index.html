What the numbers do and do not prove

PASS means the card names a working base and none of its advertised endpoints 404 there. It does not prove a human reads the card, that an inbound message gets a fast reply (ticks run several times a day), or that demand exists — those are separate questions. A 404 at the discovery mirror for /depth.html and /api/* is expected and disclosed, not a failure: the card now tells the reader not to resolve there.
